    <h3>NCSC: Using TLS to protect data (PQC update)</h3>
    <p>UK NCSC TLS guidance v1.2 adds hybrid and pure post-quantum TLS 1.3 profiles (e.g. X25519MLKEM768) following RFC 10024. Only TLS 1.3 supports PQC, so plan TLS 1.2 migration now (Oct 2026).</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/using-tls-to-protect-data">Visit source</a>
  </article>

    <h3>EN 40000-1-2:2026 product cybersecurity lifecycle</h3>
    <p>The first foundational European standard supporting the Cyber Resilience Act defines generic cybersecurity principles, baseline product-risk management requirements, and lifecycle activities from design through end of support and decommissioning (available 18 Nov 2026). Its <a href="https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2026/en-in-the-spotlight/2026-11-19-cra-standards-en-40000-1-1-and-en-40000-1-2/">announcement and scope are public</a>; the complete standard is obtained through national standards catalogues.</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://standards.cencenelec.eu/ords/f?p=205:110:0::::FSP_PROJECT,FSP_ORG_ID:81335,2307986&amp;cs=1D72BA048927BF4E6076BA309587EA01A">View catalogue entry</a>

    <h3>NIST: Design Tenets for Agent Identity Management Systems</h3>
    <p>Ten risk-based design tenets for agent identity, delegation, least privilege, revocable authorization, policy separation, monitoring, and tamper-evident evidence (Oct 2026).</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://www.nist.gov/blogs/cybersecurity-insights/design-tenets-agent-identity-management-systems">Visit source</a>

    <h3>UK regulators: AI cyber risk and resilience practices</h3>
    <p>Non-binding Bank of England, PRA, and FCA practices for AI cyber resilience cover governance, threat modeling, controlled tool access, validation, human review, zero trust, segmentation, least privilege, monitoring, and recovery (Nov 2026).</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2026/november/effective-practices-cyber-risk-and-resilience-management-relating-to-artificial-intelligence">Visit source</a>
  </article>

    <h3>CISA: Dams Sector Cybersecurity Framework guidance</h3>
    <p>Voluntary, risk-based guidance for dam owners and operators to apply cybersecurity practices according to organizational size, risk, and capability (Nov 2026). It is sector-specific and does not create additional regulatory requirements.</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/dams-sector-cybersecurity-framework-implementation-guidance">Visit source</a>
  </article>

    <h3>NIST SP 1800-42: Mobile driver's license guidance</h3>
    <p>Updated draft guidance for secure, interoperable mobile driver's licenses adds credential-revocation, same-device, quantum-risk, privacy, fraud-control, and reference-architecture material (Nov 2026). These are public-comment drafts, not final standards.</p>
    <a class="button" href="https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/11/updated-guidelines-accelerating-adoption-mobile-drivers-licenses-mdl">Visit source</a>
